## 1. Test Methodology & Protocol

The 5-second test evaluates whether an engineering hiring manager or technical recruiter can immediately understand the candidate's core identity, specialization, tech stack, and availability within a single 5-second glance without scrolling.

### Protocol Steps
1. **Exposure:** Show the homepage hero (Desktop 1440×900 or Mobile 390×844) to a peer or recruiter for exactly 5 seconds.
2. **Mask:** Minimize or blur the screen.
3. **Recall Questions:** Ask the participant the following 3 diagnostic questions immediately:
   - *Question 1 (Role):* What role does this person do? What do they build?
   - *Question 2 (Stack):* What is their primary technology stack?
   - *Question 3 (Hiring & Contact):* Are they available to hire, and where/how can you reach them?
4. **Recording:** Record their raw answers verbatim in the table below. Do not guide or correct them during the test.
